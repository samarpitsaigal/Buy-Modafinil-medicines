Why is Modafinil prescribed in Australia?

Answer: Modafinil is a wakefulness-promoting agent primarily indicated for patients diagnosed with chronic sleep disorders. It is used to manage excessive daytime sleepiness (EDS) by modulating neurotransmitters in the brain responsible for the sleep-wake cycle.
